LUDO

The classic race around the board. Roll a 6 to get started.

How to play
  • Roll a 6 to bring a token out of your base.
  • Tokens travel clockwise, then up your coloured home column to the centre.
  • Starred squares and your start square are safe.
  • Land on an opponent off a safe square to send them back to base.
  • A 6, a capture, or getting a token home gives you another roll.
  • First to bring all four tokens to the centre wins.

Milestones

Beat the computer to unlock the next level. Higher levels play smarter.

Online play is coming soon

We're still building real-time matches. For now, you can pass the device around and play with up to 3 friends.
